What we do

Complete engineered design, from first concept to final compliance.

01

Conceptualisation

Getting your project off the ground requires accurate, timely advice. We guide the early stages with pool concept design, spatial and services coordination, and operating and capital cost expenditure analyses.

02

Development

Drawing on extensive experience with aquatic facilities, we develop your concept into a complete detailed design — integrated hydraulic, structural and finishes services. Buildable, coordinated designs delivered in Revit, AutoCAD and Inventor with full BIM integration.

03

Delivery

To ensure smooth delivery throughout construction, we offer full contract administration support — shop drawing and technical submission reviews, inspections, supervision and commissioning assistance.

04

Compliance

Aquatic facilities contain high-risk, expensive capital equipment. We provide compliance services — resolving operation and maintenance problems, maintenance planning, and facility audits that enhance operator and patron safety.
